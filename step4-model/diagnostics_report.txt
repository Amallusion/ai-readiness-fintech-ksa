B. CORRELATION — differencing collapses it for every sector
====================================================================
  mean |level correlation|       = 0.901
  mean |differenced correlation| = 0.170
  range of differenced r         = [-0.238, 0.328]
  |r| for p<0.05 at n=59          = 0.256  -> 4 of 16 pass
  |r| after Bonferroni (16 tests) = 0.378  -> 0 of 16 pass
  The finding is not that Jewelry moved to mid-range. It is that the
  level correlations were measuring shared upward trend for ALL sectors
  and carry no sector-specific signal. This retires the correlation leg
  of the original 'three independent confirmations' claim entirely.
